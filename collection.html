About the prices

Each lot shows a fair price range rather than one fixed price, drawn from recent comparable sales. Completeness moves a Longaberger piece more than anything else: a basket still holding its original liner, protector, lid, tie-on and product card sits at the top of its range, a bare basket at the bottom.

Sets and lots

Some pieces are sold together as one lot because they were made to go together. Those listings show the individual prices too, so you can ask about a single piece if you would rather.

Condition and enquiries

Condition is described honestly, damage included, and the photographs show the actual lot. A few lots are still waiting to be photographed and are available all the same. Please contact the Tebbets Community Club to ask about, reserve or collect any item.
